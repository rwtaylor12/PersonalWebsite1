Built with AI · Fitness

5×5 Workout Tracker

A small app for following the StrongLifts 5×5 program. Log a session and it works out next time's weights for you: add weight after a clean workout, repeat after missed reps, and drop about 10% after three misses in a row.

  • Plate math for every lift, so you know what to load on each side of the bar
  • Progress charts for each lift, plus body weight and how you felt
  • Full history with edit and delete

Loaded with sample workouts so you can see how it works. Anything you log saves in your browser only.

For my students · Accounting I

Accounting Cycle Practice

An interactive version of the Chapter 4 comprehensive problem I built for my Principles of Accounting I class at SAU Tech. Students work one month of a new service business, from the first journal entry to the post-closing trial balance.

  • Every cell is checked as you go: green ✓ for right, red ✗ for another look
  • "New problem" creates a fresh business and numbers, with a shareable code so a whole class can work the same version
  • Journal, T accounts, trial balances, financial statements, closing entries and ratios, plus an answer key

Work saves in your browser, so you can come back to it.
